About

I am an undergraduate in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University, in the Talent Program for Intelligent Robotics. My research interests lie in world models for embodied systems, robotics, and representation learning.

I work on learning models that connect visual observations with actions and preserve spatial consistency over time. My projects span controllable video generation, multi-source representation alignment, and compositional 3D scene reconstruction.

I have worked with Prof. Yong Li at FIBLAB and Prof. Yueqi Duan at the Tsinghua Spatial Intelligence & Vision Group. I am currently an R&D intern at Shanghai Coco Matrix Intelligent Technology, working on robotic model pre-training.
